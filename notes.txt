★Core Concepts..

1.LLM(Large Language Model):-
Models like GPT that can generate text,code,or reasoning outputs.
2.SLM(Small Language Model):-
Lightweight models optimized for specific tasks,faster and cheaper than LLMs.
3.RAG(Retrieval-Augmented Generation):-
Technique where an LLM retrieves external knowledge(e.g.,from a database or documents)before generating an answer,improving accuracy.
